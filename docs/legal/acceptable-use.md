# Acceptable Use Policy

**Draft for counsel review. Not legal advice.**
**Product:** AI Inbox (name not final)
**Last updated:** October 30, 2026

This policy is part of the Terms of Service. If you use AI Inbox (the “Service”), you follow it. “You” means the person or company that holds the account. Questions and reports go to [abuse@example.com].

The Service reads mail you connect, drafts replies, and sends a reply only after you approve it. You are responsible for the mailboxes you connect and for every message you approve.

## You must have the right

Connect only mailboxes you are allowed to use. Upload only files you are allowed to use. Do not connect a coworker’s mailbox, a client’s mailbox, or a shared mailbox unless you have that right. Do not send mail that pretends to be from someone else.

## Do not misuse mail

You may not use the Service to:

- Send spam, bulk unsolicited mail, or mail people have asked not to receive.
- Phish, spread malware, or send anything that is designed to break into an account or a computer.
- Threaten, harass, or defame anyone.
- Send content that is illegal in the place you send it from or to.
- Hide who a message is from, or forge headers, in order to mislead.
- Use an AI summary or draft as if it were a fact you have not checked, when you know it is being relied on for a legal, medical, or financial decision. The Service is not professional advice. You still have to read what you send.

## Do not misuse the Service

You may not:

- Probe, scan, or attack the Service, Cloudflare, WorkOS, Microsoft, or Google, except under a written test we have agreed to.
- Bypass sign-in, the session cookie, or the rule that mail sends only after approval.
- Overload the Service, or use it to mine, scrape, or resell mailbox access.
- Copy the product’s code or design. Use of the hosted Service is not a license to the codebase. See the Copyright Notice.
- Reverse engineer the Service except where the law expressly allows it and you cannot waive that right.
- Use the Service if you are under 18.

## What we may do

If we believe you have broken this policy, the Terms, or the law, we may remove content, disconnect a mailbox, refuse a send, or suspend or close the account. We may do that with or without notice when we need to protect someone, comply with law, or protect the Service. We may keep logs needed to investigate abuse, as the Privacy Policy describes.

We are not obligated to monitor mail. We do not promise to catch misuse.

Email [abuse@example.com] with the account, the message, and what happened. For copyright claims, use the DMCA page instead. This inbox is not for product support.
