Other names are the property of their owners. Using those names here is only to say which services the product connects to.

## About the placeholder “AI Inbox”

“AI Inbox” is descriptive. It says the product is an inbox that uses AI. Descriptive phrases are weak as trademarks. They are hard to register, hard to enforce, and easy for someone else to use in ordinary speech. This notice does not create trademark rights in the words “AI Inbox,” and it does not stop another product from using similar words.

Before launch, pick a distinctive name, search it (United States Patent and Trademark Office, plus a web and app-store search), and have counsel clear it. Then replace “AI Inbox” in the product, these drafts, and this notice. Rights in the real name begin with use in commerce, and a registration makes them easier to enforce. This draft does not file an application.

## What others may not do

Once the real name is in use, others may not use it in a way that is likely to confuse people about the source of a product, or that suggests we made or endorse their product. Ordinary descriptive use, such as saying a message is in an “AI inbox” as a common phrase, is not something this notice tries to forbid.
