## Who we are

The Service is operated by [Company Name], [address]. Privacy questions go to [privacy@example.com].

## Information we collect

**Account information.** You sign in through WorkOS. We receive identifiers WorkOS provides, such as your name, email address, and a user id. We do not ask you for your mailbox password.

**Mailbox and message data.** If you connect Outlook or Gmail, we access the mail those providers allow, including messages, headers, and the content needed to rank mail, write a summary, draft a reply, and fill a form. We also store the drafts you edit, the forms and files you upload, and a record of what you approved and when.

**Usage information.** We collect basic logs of how the Service is used, such as sign-in time, pages loaded, errors, and the browser and device type. Cloudflare, which hosts the site, may also log IP address and request data as part of delivering the site.

**Saved on your device.** The browser keeps a few of your own choices, such as whether you dismissed a banner and whether this tab is in demo mode. Those values stay in the browser. See the Cookie and Tracking Disclosure.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use it for advertising.

## How we use it

We use this information to run the Service: to sign you in, show your inbox, prepare summaries and drafts, fill forms from your email and the files you uploaded, and send a reply only after you approve it. We also use it to keep the Service secure, fix errors, and meet legal duties.

An artificial-intelligence model helps produce summaries, rankings, drafts, and form fills. The model API key stays on our servers. It is not sent to your browser, and we do not put your mailbox tokens in the browser.

## Who else processes it

We share data with providers only so they can do their part of the Service:

- **WorkOS** handles sign-in and the session.
- **Microsoft** handles Outlook mail, when you connect an Outlook account.
- **Google** handles Gmail, when you connect a Gmail account.
- **[AI Provider Name]** provides the AI summaries, rankings, drafts, and form fills. AI summaries are provided by [AI Provider Name]. It receives the message content and files needed for that work. It is not allowed to use that content to advertise to you. Replace [AI Provider Name] with the real provider before launch.
- **Cloudflare** hosts and delivers the site.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users and the Service.

Connecting Outlook or Gmail is optional, but the Service cannot read or send mail for an account you have not connected. Microsoft’s and Google’s own privacy policies apply to those accounts.

## How long we keep it

We keep account and mailbox data while your account is open and you stay connected.

If you disconnect a mailbox, we delete that mailbox’s stored access and stop syncing it. Within 30 days we also delete the messages, drafts, and uploaded files we stored for that mailbox. Disconnecting one mailbox does not delete mail or files from a mailbox you leave connected.

If you close your account, we delete or de-identify your account information and the messages, drafts, and uploaded files we stored for every mailbox on that account, within 30 days.

We may keep security, fraud-prevention, and legal logs after that. Those leftover records are kept only as long as that purpose requires, and no longer than [24 months] unless the law requires more. Backups may still hold a copy of deleted mail, drafts, or files until the backup cycle overwrites it, and no longer than [90 days].

## Your choices and rights

You can disconnect Outlook or Gmail in Settings. That stops new mail from that account, deletes our stored access for it, and starts the 30-day deletion of that mailbox’s messages, drafts, and files described above. You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to export it, by emailing [privacy@example.com]. You can sign out at any time. Signing out ends your session. It does not by itself disconnect a mailbox.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to appeal a refusal, or to complain to a privacy regulator. We will not discriminate against you for making a request.

The Service is not for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including encryption in transit and access limits on the servers that hold mailbox tokens and the AI key. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, if the change is material, post a notice in the Service. Continued use after the update means you accept the revised policy.
