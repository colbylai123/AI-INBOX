What each piece is for
  Privacy policy. What we collect (account, mailbox, files, logs), why, that the AI key stays on the server, who else touches the data (WorkOS, Microsoft, Google, [AI Provider Name], Cloudflare), and what mail, drafts, and files are deleted on disconnect or account closure.
  Terms of service. The contract. As-is, no warranty, a low cap on damages with exceptions for gross negligence, willful misconduct, and a privacy or security failure caused by the company. Users cover only third-party claims they caused. The product is not professional or legal advice.
  Copyright notice. Original code, copy, and design are all rights reserved. Third-party packages keep their own licenses. Do not add an open-source license at the repo root.
  Trademark notice. Placeholder only. "AI Inbox" is descriptive and is not a registered mark. Do not use the R-in-a-circle. Swap in a real name after a search.
  IP protection summary. Copyright covers our expression, not the idea of approve-and-send. Do not claim contractor work without a signed written transfer. The name is not locked. Dependencies are not ours. Nothing here is a patent.
  Acceptable use. No mailboxes you do not own, no spam, phishing, or malware, no attacks on the service. We may suspend.
  Cookies and tracking. Honest for this release: a WorkOS session cookie set by the API, a few preferences in browser storage, Cloudflare for hosting. No advertising analytics, and no analytics vendor is installed. Update that section before one is turned on.
  DMCA page. How a copyright owner asks us to take material down. Forwarding a valid counter-notice and restoring the material are required steps, so the copyright safe harbor is not optional. The agent block is still blank.
