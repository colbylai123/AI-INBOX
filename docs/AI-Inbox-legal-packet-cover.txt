What each piece is for
  Privacy policy. What we collect (account, mailbox, files, logs), why, that the AI key stays on the server, who else touches the data (WorkOS, Microsoft, Google, the AI provider, Cloudflare), how long we keep it, and how to ask for access or deletion.
  Terms of service. The contract. Written to protect the company on a first release: as-is, no warranty, a low cap on damages, no liability for lost mail or a wrong AI summary, users cover claims that come from their use, and the product is not professional or legal advice.
  Copyright notice. Original code, copy, and design are all rights reserved. Third-party packages keep their own licenses. Do not add an open-source license at the repo root.
  Trademark notice. Placeholder only. "AI Inbox" is descriptive and is not a registered mark. Do not use the R-in-a-circle. Swap in a real name after a search.
  IP protection summary. The short version for workers: copyright covers our expression, not the idea of approve-and-send. The name is not locked. Dependencies are not ours. Nothing here is a patent.
  Acceptable use. No mailboxes you do not own, no spam, phishing, or malware, no attacks on the service. We may suspend.
  Cookies and tracking. Honest for this release: a WorkOS session cookie set by the API, a few preferences in browser storage, Cloudflare for hosting. No advertising analytics, and no analytics vendor is installed. Update that section before one is turned on.
  DMCA page. How a copyright owner asks us to take material down, and how a user sends a counter-notice. The agent block is still blank.

Before anyone publishes a page
  Fill every bracket: company legal name, address, state, county, and the privacy, legal, abuse, and DMCA emails.
  Name the AI provider in the privacy policy.
  Put the real session-cookie name, lifetime, and flags in the cookie page.
  Have a lawyer read the packet, especially the liability cap, the class-action waiver, and the DMCA agent filing.
  Do not tell anyone "AI Inbox" is trademarked.
  Do not wire these pages into the app until Colby asks.
